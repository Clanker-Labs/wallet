## The monthly routine it's built around

1. **Telegram pings you on the 1st.** Tap *Update balances now* and answer one number per account. Investment accounts update themselves from market prices, and mortgages amortize on their own. ([Reminders & Telegram](telegram.html))
2. **Drop last month's statement** (CSV or PDF) anywhere in the app, or send it to the bot. The assistant reads it, imports the transactions, skips duplicates and categorizes them. ([Assistant](assistant.html))
3. **Ask what changed:** "Where did my money go this month?" Every answer comes from your own data through the same tools you can call over MCP or HTTP. ([Agent tools](tools.html))
